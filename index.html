Most reporting problems aren’t reporting problems.

They’re data problems further upstream. A deal closed in the CRM but never invoiced. A customer spelled three ways. A spreadsheet someone updates by hand every Friday.

A better-looking dashboard won’t fix that. Connecting the systems properly, once, will. That’s the work I do: tracing where the numbers come from, fixing them at the source, and then building reports that refresh on their own.

The result is less time preparing reports, and more trust in the ones you have.

What I can do for you

Integrate your systems with each other

Make your CRM, accounting and other business systems share data through their APIs. For example, a customer or deal created in Salesforce can flow into Visma Economic, so nothing is typed twice and the systems stay in step.

Bring your data into Power BI

Connect financial systems, CRM data, databases and spreadsheets to one Power BI data model through APIs and ETL pipelines, refreshed on a schedule so nobody copies numbers by hand.

Build Power BI dashboards

Finance, sales and operations dashboards designed around the questions your team actually asks, with clear definitions behind every figure.

Automate recurring reports

Month-end packs and weekly sales reports that refresh on schedule and land in inboxes, instead of being rebuilt each time.

Clean up the data

Duplicates, inconsistent names and missing fields found and fixed, plus simple rules in Salesforce so the mess doesn’t come back.
